#### Title
**Part 1: Work / Study 话题**

#### Common questions
- What do you do? / Do you work or are you a student?
- Why did you choose that job / subject?
- Do you like it? Why?
- Would you like to change your job / subject?

#### 7.0+ 答案结构(每个问题 2-3 句)
1. **直接回答**(Yes / No / I... / I'm...)
2. **解释 / 例子**(because... / for example...)
3. **延伸**(but... / actually... / I think...)

#### 7.0+ 答案示例
Q: "What do you do?"
A: "I'm currently a third-year student majoring in Mechanical Engineering at Nanjing University of Science and Technology. **I chose this subject** because I've always been fascinated by how things work — ever since I was a kid, **I would** take apart toys to see their mechanisms. **Although** the coursework can be challenging at times, **I find it** incredibly rewarding."

#### Useful vocabulary
- 职位: engineer, designer, manager, consultant, teacher, researcher
- 公司: a multinational company, a start-up, a state-owned enterprise
- 学科: major in, specialize in, focus on
- 频率: full-time, part-time, freelance

#### Common errors
- 答案太短("I'm a student." → 只 1 句,6.0 封顶)
- 没展开原因 / 例子
- 模板化(背答案,考官能听出来)

#### Related KPs
- kp_ielts_speaking_s_fluency(流利度技能)
- kp_ielts_speaking_s_grammar(语法技能)

## 3. Part 2 话题卡片(20 KP)

### Part 2 概述
- 3-4 分钟
- 1 张话题卡(Describe a...) + 1 分钟准备 + 2 分钟独白
- 必须说满 2 分钟(短了扣分)

### 4 大类别

| # | 类别 | KP 数量 |
|---|------|---------|
| 1 | 人物 (a person you admire / a friend / a family member) | 5 |
| 2 | 事件 (a memorable event / a happy event / an achievement) | 5 |
| 3 | 地点 (a place you like / a place you visited / a building) | 5 |
| 4 | 物品 (an object / a gift / a piece of technology) | 5 |

#### Title
**Part 2: Describe a person 描述人物**

#### 答题结构(2 分钟,5-7 句)
1. **Who** the person is
2. **How you know** them
3. **What they look like / personality**
4. **Why they influenced you**
5. **Specific example** of influence
6. **How you feel about them now**

#### 7.0+ 答案示例(2 分钟)
```
I'd like to talk about my high school physics teacher, Mr. Wang, who has had a profound influence on my academic journey.

I first met Mr. Wang when I was 15, in my second year of high school. He was in his late forties, tall and lean, with kind eyes behind his glasses. What struck me most was his patience — he never got frustrated with our endless questions, no matter how silly they seemed.

Mr. Wang influenced me in two main ways. First, he taught me to think critically rather than just memorize formulas. He would always ask "why" and "how" in class, pushing us to understand the underlying principles. This way of thinking has stayed with me throughout my university studies. Second, he showed me the joy of learning itself. He often stayed after class to discuss topics beyond the textbook, and his enthusiasm was contagious.

Looking back, I realize that Mr. Wang didn't just teach me physics — he taught me how to learn, and that's a gift I'll carry forever.
```

#### Useful structures
- "I'd like to talk about..."
- "What struck me most was..."
- "...has influenced me in [N] main ways."
- "First... Second... Third..."
- "Looking back, I realize that..."

#### Common errors
- 说不够 2 分钟(准备了但紧张说短了)
- 卡顿(忘了某个点)
- 缺少具体例子

#### Related KPs
- 准备 1 分钟策略
- 卡片关键词利用(避免写完整答案)

#### Title
**Part 3: Education 抽象讨论**

#### Common questions
- How has education changed in your country in the last 20 years?
- Do you think the current education system prepares students well for work?
- What are the advantages and disadvantages of online education?
- Should university education be free for everyone?

#### 7.0+ 答题结构(4-6 句)
1. **明确观点**(Yes / No / I believe...)
2. **理由 1** + 例子
3. **理由 2** + 例子
4. **让步 / 反方观点**("However, some people argue...")
5. **总结**

#### 7.0+ 答案示例
Q: "Do you think the current education system prepares students well for work?"

A: "I think it depends on the country, but generally, I believe traditional education focuses too much on rote memorization and exam scores, rather than practical skills. For example, many graduates I know struggle with basic financial planning, public speaking, and teamwork — skills that are essential in modern workplaces. However, I should mention that some schools are now introducing project-based learning, which I think is a step in the right direction. That said, there's still a long way to go before our education system truly prepares students for real-world challenges."

#### Useful structures
- "I think it depends on..."
- "I believe [claim], because [reason]."
- "For example,..."
- "However, [反方]..."
- "That said,..."

#### Common errors
- 答案太短(2-3 句)
- 缺乏例子
- 没有反方观点(显得片面)
- 用 too simple 词汇(very, really, so much)

#### Related KPs
- kp_ielts_speaking_s_opinion(观点表达)
- kp_ielts_speaking_s_example(举例技能)
